This manual covers PetaNuri3D v1.3.0. Keys are written with their default assignments (if you reassign keys, the explanations inside the app show your current keys). Features marked Standard are available in Standard and Pro, and features marked Pro are available in Pro. Features with no badge are available from the free Lite edition.

What has changed is listed in the Changelog, and the status of requests we have received is in Requests and status.
