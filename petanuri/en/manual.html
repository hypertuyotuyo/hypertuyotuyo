PetaNuri3D manual

New users: start with “Getting started,” then “Drawing.”Standard features are available in Standard and Pro; Pro pages and features require Pro. Unmarked features are available in the free Lite edition.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

This manual covers PetaNuri3D v1.1.0. Keys shown are the default bindings (after rebinding, in-app tips show your current keys).

Getting started

Launch, tutorial, samples, new projects, saving/autosave, reloading models, editions and upgrading to Pro

Draw

Tools/sub tools, back-face painting, brush settings, blending, fill, color/opacity gradients, transparent color, shapes, colors and symmetry (supported tools)

Stamps, patterns, surface relief and 2D canvas

Decorations, stamps, pattern pens for fabric, surface relief for seams/wrinkles, stamp 2D canvas and 2D painting

Selections, transforms, text and rulers

Selections, move/transform tool, transforms in 3D (free transform/warp), pasted images, text and rulers

Layer

Layers panel, folders, masks, layer properties, strokes, adjustment layers, filters, auto fill and history

UV view and visible area

Paint directly in a side-by-side UV view, grid/snapping, seam-continuous painting, visible area, Hide pen, visibility presets and mesh lines

Interface, panels and preferences

Default layout, moving panels, toolbar, favorite tools, the four corners of the 3D view, light direction, right-click menus and preferences

Texture sets and objects

Select texture sets, main/sub sets, resolution, replacing the base, object visibility and memory savings

Final look and lilToon materials Pro

Preview lilToon (beta), paint shadow colors/masks, separate materials, import .mat and export .unitypackage

Bake AO Pro

Calculate darkness where light is blocked from model geometry and create a layer (also per object)

Easy Bake, materials and shading Pro

12 finishes for skin/outfits/accessories, blush points, shading, gradients, cavities, rim light, highlights, fabric texture, cast shadows and translucency color

Poses, blend shapes and vertex groups Pro

Paint in poses, humanoid bone colors, pose symmetry, paint with expressions and select from bone weights

Export

PNG, PSD, export padding, output folders and materials

Paint from Unity and AI integration

Select an avatar in Unity to paint (saving updates Unity); AI integration (MCP)

Shortcuts and pen controls

Shortcut list (F1), pen/mouse controls, views, key list, rebinding and troubleshooting pen pressure
