Interface, panels and preferences

Move panels and toolbars wherever you like. Layout is restored on the next launch.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Lite, Standard, Pro, and the Pro upgrade are available on BOOTH.

Default layout

  • Left: Sub tool, Tool properties, Brush size, Reference image.
  • Right, three columns: Texture sets, Layer properties and Layers beside 3D; Color, Color values, Color set/Recent colors and History next; Objects and Pro panels (Vertex groups, Material, Blend shapes, Pose) collapsed into an icon strip at the far right.
  • Column widths adapt to window size. Below 1400px wide (1280×720, 1366×768 etc.), color panels also join the far-right icon strip to leave more 3D space.
  • The default layout rearranges on resize. After customizing, it stays as arranged. View → Window → Reset window layout restores the default.

Move panels

  • Drag a panel header; release to place it.
  • Middle of a column/row: insert there. Edge: create a neighboring column/row. Edge of 3D (Add column to left etc.): create one on that side. Elsewhere: float.
  • Drag boundaries to change width/height (double-click to reset). Side-column panels also have bottom handles for height.
  • Floating panels move by their headers and resize with bottom-right handles. Show/hide through View (or header right-click → Hide this panel). Panels remain inside the app window.
Floating windows can be moved and resized by their headers even while “Lock panel layout” is on. The lock at the upper right of each one fixes or unfixes just that window. View → Windows → Toolbox shows a panel with the tool icons in a grid (with foreground and background colors below).

Tabs, collapsing and icon strips

  • Collapse: Click the header to show only the header. Collapsing unused panels gives others more room.
  • Group as tabs: Drag a header onto another panel's header (release when Drop here to group as tabs appears). Drag a tab out to separate it.
  • Icon strip: Use “<”“>” at the column top to collapse it into a narrow icon strip. Click an icon to open only that panel beside it (click outside or press Esc to close).

Toolbar and favorite tools

  • Placement: Drag the toolbar handle to show docking zones along all four workspace edges; release at an edge (horizontal at top/bottom). Release elsewhere to float. Also choose via button right-click → Toolbar placement.
  • Icon size: Right-click a button → Toolbar icon size → Large/Small.
  • Theme color: The sun/moon toggle at the toolbar end switches Light/Dark.
  • Reorder tools: Drag buttons into your preferred order, including with a pen. Move then release to reorder; release without moving to select. Right-click → Reset order restores the default.
  • Favorite tools: Right-click a toolbar button or sub tool → Add to favorites. They appear in small strips above 3D and UV; click to switch. + adds the current sub tool (pen-only supported).
File is now the leftmost menu, followed by New, Open, Save, Undo, and Redo buttons (dimmed when unavailable). The edition is shown in the window title.
Favorite tool bars for the 3D view and UV view can be shown or hidden separately (View → Windows). While Lock panel layout in View → Windows is on, panels are not moved or resized.
Long menus and context menus that do not fit on screen scroll when you hold the mouse or pen at the top or bottom edge (▲/▼ marks show more items). The wheel and arrow keys also scroll them.

The four corners of the 3D view

  • Top left: Mesh lines, display mode, single/double-sided, symmetry X/Y/Z, visible area (icons with ▼ open settings below).
  • Top center: Paint/Pose toggle (bone controls in Pose; paint-target controls while painting a target). Drag the left ⋮ handle to reposition.
  • Top right: Front, Back, Right, Left, Top, Bottom, Perspective/Orthographic, Frame all (numpad also works).
  • Bottom right: 3D navigator and alternate-angle mini view (a small 3D view from another camera; view-only).
  • Bottom left: Switch to touched set, main/sub texture sets, paint across sets (Texture set).
  • Bottom center: Selection bar and hidden-area bar.
  • When 3D is narrow, icons move into the ⋯ popup starting from the top right.
The ▼ under the perspective/orthographic icon at the upper right sets the perspective field of view from 5° to 120°. Smaller values are more telephoto, larger values wider (default 30°). The value persists after restart.
3D navigator (all editions): the navigator now has the sphere with Frame all and perspective/orthographic below it on the left, the magnifier, hand, and view rotation on the right, and ⟲, angle, ⟳ along the bottom (the same layout as the UV guide).
View rotation (all editions): tilt the 3D view like paper while painting. Shift+wheel rotates 5°, ⟲/⟳ rotate 15° (click the angle for 0°), or drag the rotate button left or right (double-click for 0°). Keys can be assigned to “Rotate view left”, “Rotate view right”, and “Reset view rotation”.

Display modes and light direction

  • Display modes (3D top left): Unlit painted colors, Two-tone toon, Shaded (lit to reveal shape), Final look (lilToon; Pro). Restored on next launch.
  • Use ▼ below the display-mode icon for Light direction: Drag the sphere to set light direction, use azimuth/elevation sliders, or enable Follows camera (illumination stays consistent while rotating). Two-tone toon, Shaded and Final look share the light.
Wireframe only, at the right end of the display modes, hides textures and shows only mesh lines (also in View → Display mode). The lines follow poses, blend shapes, and moves.
Show as pixels (all editions): shows textures in 3D without interpolation, each texel as a square (display only; your painting and exports don’t change). Toggle it per texture set with “Show as pixels (no interpolation)” in the set row’s ⋯ or right-click menu. Sets of 256px or less are on by default. View → Display mode → Show all sets’ textures as pixels (no interpolation) toggles all sets at once.
Transparent areas (erased or painted with the transparent color) now show a checkerboard in 3D (toggle with View → Display mode → Show transparent areas as a checkerboard; off shows gray). Right-click a texture set (…) → Alpha cutout (hide transparency in 3D) to view it as Cutout or Translucent (display only; exported images are unchanged).

Right-click menu

Menus suit the clicked area (3D, UV, layers, texture sets, sub tools, color sets, history, panel headers etc.). In 3D, quickly pick color, select set/layer painted here, show only this island, hide object, choose views or symmetry.

Menu items show icons at left and current keys at right. ▶ opens submenus; navigate with ↑↓←→・Enter・Esc too.

Preferences

File → Preferences (Ctrl＋Alt＋Shift＋K). Categories appear left; collapsible sections right. Hover over setting names for descriptions.

Settings controlled elsewhere are absent from Preferences: Paint across two sets (3D bottom left), continue past UV island edges (UV header), export padding/destination (export dialog), symmetry search range (symmetry ▼).
Camera controls: in File → Preferences → Controls → Camera, use Camera control template to choose right-button, middle-button, Alt-button, or Unity-style controls. Your choice is kept on restart. Space controls and the wheel remain available.
Middle-button: middle-drag rotates, Shift+middle-drag pans, and Ctrl+middle-drag zooms. Alt-button: Alt+left rotates, Alt+middle pans, and Alt+right zooms in 3D.
Read longer explanations using the round “?” icons. Hover to show help, or click or tap to keep it open. Click again, click outside, or press Esc to close it. With the keyboard, use Tab to focus “?” and Enter or Space to toggle help. Point placement steps, current status, and warnings remain on screen.
Unity style: right-drag rotates and middle-drag pans. Alt+left rotates, Alt+middle pans, and Alt+right zooms (3D view). Use I for the 3D eyedropper.
Color scheme: Preferences → Interface lets you change the window background, panel, button, text, main, secondary, 3D background, and toolbar colors. Light and dark are saved separately and can be reset to the defaults.
Pen press: menu items and buttons can be confirmed by pressing and releasing the pen (on by default). For long presses on buttons, choose confirm on release (default) or open the right-click menu. Elsewhere, such as layer rows, a long press always opens the right-click menu.
Orbit around the last painted spot (all editions): turn it on in File → Preferences → Controls → Camera (off by default) to orbit around the end of your last stroke in the 3D view. When painting details up close, the spot no longer drifts off screen as you rotate.
