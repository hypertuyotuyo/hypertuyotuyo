Glossary

What the terms used in the PetaNuri3D interface and in this manual mean. In every entry, the first sentence is the definition. For details on how to use something, follow the link at the end of the entry.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

0–9ABCEFHILMNOPRSTUV

0–9

2D canvas and 2D painting
A flat, square place to draw in 2D without using a 3D model. There are the "2D canvas for stamps", for making stamp images, and "2D painting", for drawing without a model. → 2D canvas for stamps, 2D painting

A

Adjustment layer Standard
A layer that applies corrections such as hue and brightness without rewriting the artwork below it. You can change its values afterward. → Adjustment layers
Also paint back and hidden faces
A setting that decides whether to also paint the back and deeper faces you can't see, as long as the brush reaches them. It sits right below Opacity in tool properties and is remembered for each sub tool. → Also paint the back
AO Pro
The darkness of places that light hardly reaches (recesses, overlaps and gaps). PetaNuri3D calculates it from the shape of the model and bakes it into a layer. → Bake AO
Auto fill
A feature that creates a base coat for each area enclosed by lines, from line art and color "hints" (Layers → Auto fill). → Auto fill
Autosave
A mechanism that saves your work automatically every 5 minutes. Up to the 3 newest saves are kept for each project, and you can restore one with File → Open from autosave. → Save and resume

B

Bleed (padding)
When you export, extending the edge colors outside the UV islands. If you don't, lines may show at the seams when the texture is scaled down (the default is 8px). → Bleed
Blend
A tool for blurring, smudging and mixing colors that are already painted (key J). Its sub tools include "Blur", "Smudge" and "Blend". → Blend
Blend mode
The rule for how a layer's artwork is combined with the artwork below it (28 kinds, including Normal, Multiply, Screen and Overlay). → Blend mode
Blend shapes Pro
Changes in the shape of the model, such as facial expressions. You can set the model to that shape and then paint. → Blend shapes

C

Clipping
A setting that shows a layer only where the layer below it has paint. Use it to lay down shadows and patterns without going over the edge. → Clipping

E

Easy Bake Pro
A feature that lets you pick from 12 finishes for skin, outfits and accessories and creates shading and gradient base coats all at once. → Easy Bake
Editions (Lite, Standard, Pro)
The three kinds of PetaNuri3D. In this manual, features in Standard and above carry a Standard badge, and Pro-only features carry a Pro badge. → Editions
Export
Creating texture images (PNG, PSD) and materials in a form that other apps can use. It is a separate operation from saving the project file. → Export

F

Filters Standard
A feature that applies color adjustment, blur, noise and so on to the artwork of the selected layer itself (the "Filters" menu). → Filters
Final look Pro
One of the display modes of the 3D view. It uses the same calculation as lilToon to show the look with shadows, rim light and so on added (beta). → Final look
Foreground and background colors
The foreground color is the color you paint with, and the background color is another color kept in reserve. Press X to swap them and D to reset them to black and white. → Choosing colors

H

Hide pen
A tool that hides the faces or islands you trace from the 3D display. If you hide the front parts, the faces behind them that overlapped and couldn't be painted become paintable. Alt+H brings everything back. → Hide pen
History
A panel that lists the operations you can undo, oldest first. Click a row to go back at once to just after that operation (up to 30 steps). → History

I

Island (UV island)
A group of faces that are connected to each other on the UV. The "Fill island" option of the Fill tool paints the whole island under the point you click. → Show UV and paint directly

L

Layer
Each sheet of artwork, stacked like transparent film. Every texture set has a base layer at the very bottom. → Layers
Layer properties
A panel that lists the settings of the selected layer. You also change the values of adjustment layers and turn Stroke on and off here. → Layer properties
lilToon
A Unity shader (the mechanism that calculates how things look) widely used for VRChat avatars. In Pro, you can check your work in that look and import and export materials. → Final look and lilToon materials
Lock transparency
A setting that lets you draw only where the layer already has paint, and not on transparent areas. Use it to repaint colors you've already painted without changing their shapes. → Lock transparency

M

Main and sub (texture sets)
The two texture sets that are ready to paint on right away. The set you've selected is the main one, the set you selected just before it is the sub one, and you can paint across both with a single stroke. → Paint across two sets
Mask (layer mask)
Something you paint in black and white to decide which parts of a layer to show and which to hide. It hides parts without erasing the artwork itself (white = show, black = hide). → Layer mask
MCP (AI integration)
A mechanism that lets an AI app operate PetaNuri3D. Turn it on in File → Preferences → AI integration (it is off by default). → AI integration
Mesh wireframe
A display that overlays the edges of the model's faces on the 3D view. Showing only the edges of islands is the "Seam lines" display. → Mesh wireframe
MToon Pro
A shader used by VRM models. In Pro, you can check how your work will look with it (beta). → Final look and lilToon materials

N

Normal map
An image that changes the direction of the surface for each pixel to make it look bumpy. The wrinkles and puffiness of stitches are drawn by height in the Normal map folder (Pro). → Stitch

O

Object
A part of the model, such as the mesh of hair, clothes or an accessory. In the Objects panel, you can hide each part or show only one. → Objects
Orphaned sets
A texture set for a material that doesn't exist in the new model after you reload the model. It isn't deleted but stays, marked with !, and you can move its layers to other sets. → Orphaned sets

P

Paint symmetrically
A feature that paints the same thing on the mirrored opposite side when you paint on one side. There are X (left/right), Y (front/back) and Z (up/down), and M toggles X. → Paint symmetrically
Paint target Pro
The place you draw to when you paint, with your usual tools, on lilToon textures other than the main color, such as shadow color, masks and normal maps. They appear as folders in the Layers panel. → Paint target
Panel
A window on the screen that holds things like color, layers and tool properties. You can move panels, group them into tabs, float them or collapse them. → Move panels
Pattern
A pen that paints only the pattern areas, using the foreground color (15 kinds, including Vertical stripes, Polka dots and Gingham). → Pattern
Popup palette
A small window that appears when you release the pen's side button (the right button) without moving it. You can choose tools and colors with the pen alone. → Popup palette
Pose mode Pro
A mode where you move bones to pose the model and paint in that shape. Use it for places that are hard to paint in the original pose, such as under the arms. → Pose mode
Project file (.petanuri)
The file that saves your work in PetaNuri3D. The model, all the layers and the settings are stored in one file, and you can open it to keep painting. → Save and resume
PSD Standard
An image format that can be saved with the layers kept separate. In PetaNuri3D, you can use it as the base when you create a new project, and for export (Standard and above). → Export as PSD

R

Resolution
The number of pixels along one side of a texture. In New, you choose from 32 to 4096 for each texture set (Lite goes up to 2048). → Change the resolution
Rest layers
A mechanism that takes layers you haven't touched for a while out of GPU memory, without changing how they look. It helps when you stack many 4096 layers (on by default). → Rest layers
Right-click menu
A list of the operations available at that spot, shown when you right-click with the mouse. The contents change in the 3D view, the layers, the texture sets and so on. → Right-click menu

S

Seam
A place that is connected in 3D but separated on the UV (the edge of an island). Toggle the display of seam lines with Alt+W. → Seam lines
Selection
An area you set by enclosing the places where you can draw. While a selection exists, you can draw only inside it. → Selection
Shading
The kinds of look the 3D view can have (painted colors as is, two-tone toon, shaded, final look and so on). Switch them at the top left of the 3D view. → The four corners of the 3D view
Stroke Standard
An effect that adds a border when layers are composited, without changing the layer's artwork. You can adjust its width and color afterward, or switch it off. → Stroke
Sub tool
A tool with different settings inside a single tool (for the Pen, for example, G-pen, Round pen and Fineliner). You can duplicate one to make your own settings. → Sub tool

T

Texture
An image applied to the surface of a 3D model. In PetaNuri3D, what you paint on the model is written into the texture. → Export
Texture set
A single texture, with its group of layers, that exists for each material. Materials that use the same image are combined into one set. → Texture sets
Tool properties
The settings of the selected sub tool (size, opacity, hardness, stabilization, pen pressure and so on). They are remembered for each sub tool. → Detailed brush settings

U

UV
A layout map that decides where each face of a 3D model corresponds on a flat texture. PetaNuri3D can show the UV next to the 3D view, and you can paint on it too. → Show UV and paint directly

V

Visible area
A mechanism that shows only the islands or faces you choose in 3D and hides the rest. You can see, and paint, the faces deep behind the hidden parts. → Visible area
