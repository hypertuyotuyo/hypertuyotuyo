Stamps, patterns, stitches and 2D canvas

These are tools for details you repeat a lot: decorations, images, clothing patterns and seams. This page also covers the 2D canvas, where you draw a picture to turn into a stamp, and 2D painting without a model.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Start with this
  1. To scatter decorations, click "Decoration" on the toolbar, choose Sparkle or another decoration, and drag over the 3D view (Details).
  2. To place an image, add a PNG with "Import image" under "Stamp", then click in the 3D view (Details).
  3. For a clothing pattern, choose "Pattern". For seams, choose "Stitch". Then drag with the foreground color (Pattern, Stitch).
  4. To turn your own drawing into a stamp, click "Draw in 2D canvas" under Stamp, draw, and click "Register as stamp" (Details).
  5. To draw without a model, start from "Start a 2D painting" on the start screen (Details).

Scatter decorations (Decoration)

Scatters small decorations in the foreground color wherever you drag. Size and direction vary slightly from one to the next.

  1. Click "Decoration" on the toolbar.
  2. Choose a decoration from the sub tools.
  3. Choose a foreground color and drag over the 3D view.
  • The decorations are Sparkle, Star, Heart, Flower, Cherry blossom, Music note, Snowflake, Polka dots, Bubble, Crescent moon, Diamond, Four-leaf clover and Drop, plus four kinds of Lace.
  • Hover over the 3D or UV view to see the shape of the tip.
  • Change Size, Scatter, Random rotation and other settings in Tool properties, as with the Pen (Set how it feels).
  • "Also paint back and hidden faces" is off by default. Symmetry also works.

Draw lace

"Lace (scallop)", "Lace (picot)", "Lace (small flowers)" and "Lace (mesh)" line up a pattern like a band along your stroke. The pattern stays connected around curves and sharp corners, and also on the 3D model.

  • The holes are transparent, and the lace is painted in the foreground color. The thickness sets the size of the pattern.
  • If you choose one as the tip of the Shape tool, you can draw lace along the outline of a rectangle or an ellipse (Shape).

Place an image (Stamp)

Places an imported image on the 3D model. Use it for logos, patches, pattern materials and so on.

  1. Click "Stamp" on the toolbar.
  2. In Tool properties, click "Import image" to add PNG, JPEG or other images (as many as you like).
  3. Choose an image from the list.
  4. Click in the 3D view to place one copy of the image. Drag to keep placing copies at the set spacing.
  • Hover over the 3D or UV view to see the image you are about to place as a translucent preview, with its current size, angle, color and opacity. The preview is only a guide before you paint. It is not added to the texture.
  • A faint preview of the next placement also appears ahead, in the direction you are moving. Turn off "Show the next placement faintly" to see only the current preview.
  • The image is placed in its own colors (transparent areas are not placed). Turn on "Paint with foreground color" to use only the image's shape and paint it in the foreground color.
  • The "Stamp (scatter)" sub tool scatters images with varied directions and positions.
  • It applies only to visible faces, and one stroke counts as one Undo step. You can also place stamps in the UV view.
  • Symmetry works in both the 3D and UV views. On the opposite side, the image is mirrored too.

Stamp settings

At the top of Tool properties, you will find a large preview of the current image, the image list, the size and the angle.

  • Imported images are kept the next time you start the app (up to 60). Use "Remove this image" to take one off the list.
  • You can also turn your own drawings into stamps (2D canvas).

If it doesn't work

  • Nothing is placed when I click: No image is selected. Add one with "Import image" and choose it from the list. Click on a visible face of the model.
  • The white box around the image is placed too: The image has no transparent area. Use a PNG with a transparent background.
  • The result differs slightly from the preview: Random rotation, Scatter and the variation settings are not shown in the preview.

Paint a clothing pattern (Pattern)

A pen that paints only the pattern areas in the foreground color. The pattern repeats on the texture, so it does not shift when you move the view.

  1. Click "Pattern" on the toolbar.
  2. Choose a pattern from the sub tools or from the swatch list at the top of Tool properties.
  3. Choose a foreground color and drag over the clothing.
  • There are 15 patterns: Vertical stripes, Horizontal stripes, Diagonal stripes, Polka dots, Small polka dots, Checkerboard, Gingham, Tartan, Argyle, Herringbone, Denim (twill), Knit, Heart, Star and Crosshatch.
  • Hover over the 3D or UV view to see the pattern to be painted, shown in the foreground color and translucent inside the brush circle.
  • With "Load pattern image", you can use any image as a pattern (brighter areas take more paint).
  • The same patterns are also available from "Paper texture" on other pens and brushes (Paper texture).

If it doesn't work

  • The pattern is upside down or sideways: The UV island is upside down or sideways. Match the direction with "Pattern angle" before you paint.
  • The pattern is distorted: Where the UVs are distorted, the pattern is distorted too.

Draw seams (Stitch)

Draws decorations that repeat along a line, such as stitches. The stitches rotate to follow the direction of the line.

  1. Click "Stitch" on the toolbar.
  2. Choose a type of stitch from the sub tools.
  3. Choose a foreground color and drag where you want stitches.
Sub toolWhat it draws
Stitches (dotted), Stitch (puffy), Seam, Piping, Cross stitch, Zigzag stitch, Studs (rivets)Draw stitches on the current layer in the foreground color. They also work in the 2D canvas
Wrinkles (soft groove), Bulge (soft raise) ProDraw relief by height in the normal map folder
  • The size basis is fixed to "Variable on screen".
  • If you choose Stitch as the tip of the Shape tool, you can line up stitches along the outline of a rectangle or a curve (Shape).

Turn seams into relief (normal map) Pro

  1. Turn on "Draw in the normal map folder" in Tool properties.
  2. Set the height with "Height (0 carve, 50 flat, 100 raise)" (shown only when the option is on).
  3. Drag over the model. The stroke goes into the "Normal map" folder in the Layers panel (the folder is created if it does not exist) and is drawn by height.
  4. When you are done, click "◀ Back to previous layer" at the top center of the 3D view. You return to the layer, display and tool you had before.
  • Wrinkles and Bulge always draw by height in the normal map folder.
  • The relief shows in the final look. If it is hard to see, raise "Normal map strength" in the Material panel.
  • When you export, it becomes a normal map image (Paint target).

Turn your drawing into a stamp (2D canvas)

On a square canvas for stamps, you draw with the usual tools and layers and can register the result as a stamp as is.

  1. Click "Stamp" on the toolbar.
  2. Click "Draw in 2D canvas" in Tool properties. The 3D view is hidden, and a square canvas appears in the UV view.
  3. Draw with the Pen, Eraser, Fill, Gradient and so on.
  4. Click "Register as stamp" at the bottom and enter a name. The combined look of the visible layers becomes the stamp as is.
  5. Go back to editing the model and paint with that stamp.
  • Layers, folders, masks, selections and Undo work as usual. The limits for each edition are the same too (Lite allows up to 4 layers).
  • Choose the canvas resolution in the field at the bottom (32 to 4096; Lite goes up to 2048).
  • To go back without registering, click "Return to model editing". What you drew is kept.
  • To edit a registered stamp, click "Redraw this image" in the Stamp tool properties, or right-click an image in the list → Redraw in 2D canvas. When you register again, choose "Overwrite" or "Register under another name".
  • You can also open it with a right-click in the list → Draw new in 2D canvas.
  • The canvas layers are saved with the project file (.petanuri) and with Autosave.
The Texture sets panel shows that you are editing a 2D canvas. This canvas is not included in the model's list of texture sets, nor in PNG or PSD exports.

Draw in 2D without a model (2D painting)

A mode for drawing on a square canvas without opening a 3D model. You start from a transparent base and draw with the usual tools and layers.

  1. Click "Start a 2D painting" on the start screen (it is below "Try the sample").
  2. In the "Create a new 2D painting" window, choose the canvas size (32 × 32 to 4096 × 4096).
  3. Draw with the Pen, Brush, Gradient and so on.
  4. Click "Export PNG" at the bottom to save the combined visible layers as a PNG with transparency.
  • If you save to a project file (.petanuri), you can keep painting later as a 2D painting, with the layers intact.
  • "Register as stamp" also works. You can keep painting in 2D painting after you register.
  • Standard File → Export → Export PSD (selected set) saves a PSD with the layers and folders intact.
  • Lite is limited to a resolution of 2048 and 4 layers, including the base. Standard and Pro go up to 4096, with no limit on the number of layers.
The 3D view and features that use the model's shape (baking, poses, materials and so on) are not available.
