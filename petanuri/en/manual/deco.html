Stamps, patterns, surface relief and 2D canvas

Tools for repetitive details such as decorations, patterns and seams. Preview placement in 3D before painting with any of them.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Notes beginning with “v1.2.0:” on this page are a release preview. The currently available version is v1.1.2.

On this page
  • Decoration
  • Stamp
  • Pattern pens (clothing patterns)
  • Surface relief (seams and wrinkles)
  • 2D canvas for stamps
  • 2D painting without a model

Decoration

  • Scatter sparkles, stars, hearts, flowers, cherry blossoms, musical notes, snowflakes, dots, bubbles, crescents, diamonds, clovers and drops along your stroke. Size and direction vary slightly.
  • Paints in the foreground color. Hover over 3D or UV to preview the tip shape.

Stamp

  • Use “Import image” in Tool properties to add PNGs or other images. Select one from the list and click the model to stamp it. Drag to place repeated stamps at the set spacing.
  • A large current-image preview, image list, size and angle appear at the top of Tool properties.
  • Hover over 3D or UV to see a translucent preview at the actual size, angle, color and opacity (preview only; not written to the texture). A faint preview of the next stamp appears ahead in your movement direction.
  • Uses the image's colors; transparent areas are skipped. Enable “Use foreground color” to use only its shape and paint in the foreground color.
  • Size, opacity, spacing, angle, random rotation, size variation, scatter, stroke-direction rotation and pressure-controlled size. Imported images persist across restarts (up to 60).
  • Symmetry works in both 3D and UV; the mirrored image is flipped too.

Pattern pens (clothing patterns)

  • 15 types: stripes, horizontal stripes, diagonal stripes, dots, small dots, checkerboard, gingham, tartan, argyle, herringbone, denim/twill, knit, hearts, stars and crosshatch.
  • A pen that paints only the pattern in the foreground color. Patterns repeat on the texture and stay fixed as the view moves (distorted UVs also distort the pattern).
  • Pattern previews, “Pattern size,” “Pattern strength,” invert light/dark and application mode appear at the top of Tool properties.
  • Hover over 3D or UV to preview the pattern translucently inside the brush circle at its actual size. While adjusting Pattern size, a larger preview circle appears at the last hovered position.
  • The same patterns are available under Paper texture → Pattern for other pens and brushes.
v1.2.0: Pattern angle (all editions): rotate the pattern of pattern pens and paper texture with “Pattern angle” in Tool properties (useful for upside-down or sideways islands).

Surface relief (seams and wrinkles) Pro

  • 9 types: recessed dashed seams, raised seams, seam grooves, raised piping, cross-stitch, zigzag stitches, rivets, wrinkles and bulges.
  • Drawing switches into the “Normal map” folder in Layers (created if needed), painting at the configured height. It does not leave black seams on color layers. Change height in Tool properties.
  • After drawing, click “◀ Return to original layer” at the top center of the 3D view to restore the previous layer, display and tool.
  • Relief appears in Final look (if faint, increase normal-map strength in the Material panel). Exports as a normal-map image (Paint target).

2D canvas for stamps

In Stamp Tool properties, click “Draw in 2D canvas” to open a dedicated stamp texture set. The 3D view is hidden; edit a square canvas in the UV view.

  1. Draw with pens, erasers, fill, gradients and more. Use regular layers, folders, selections, Undo and Redo.
  2. Choose square resolution at the bottom (256–4096; Lite up to 2048).
  3. “Register as stamp” lets you name it. The composited visible layers become the stamp. Return to model editing and draw with it.
  4. To return without registering, click “Return to model editing.” Painted layers are saved with your .petanuri project.
  • The Texture sets panel indicates stamp-canvas editing. It is handled separately from the model's main/sub assignments.
  • This working set is separate from the model's texture-set list and is excluded from model texture exports.
  • Edit registered stamps using “Redraw this image” or right-click a list entry → “Redraw in 2D canvas.” When registering again, choose overwrite or a different name.

2D painting without a model

On the startup screen below “Try a sample,” click “Start a 2D painting” and choose a resolution. This bonus painting mode provides a square transparent canvas without a 3D model. It is not selected through the New project's model settings.

  • Paint on layers, then save and resume as .petanuri.
  • “Export PNG” below the canvas saves a composite of visible layers.
  • “Register as stamp” is available too; you can continue 2D painting after registering.
  • Lite supports up to 2048 resolution and 4 layers including the base. Standard and Pro support up to 4096 resolution.
Bakes that use 3D geometry, poses and materials are not used in 2D painting.
