Troubleshooting

Common problems are listed so you can find them by symptom. The link on each line leads to the cause and the fix. If yours isn't here, try typing the symptom into the search box above.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Start with this
  1. If you make a mistake, press Ctrl+Z to go back one step. To go back several steps, use History.
  2. If the app crashed or froze, reopen it and choose File → Open from autosave (details).
  3. If you can't paint, first check which layer is selected and whether a selection is left over (Can't paint).
  4. If it still isn't fixed, you can send us a message from Help → "Contact (support, feature requests, bug reports)" (Contact us).

Starting up and opening a model

  • "Windows protected your PC" appears: The app isn't code-signed, so this can appear the first time. Click "More info", then "Run anyway" to start it (Install and launch).
  • Dropping a model on the window doesn't open it: Once a model is open, dropping another one does nothing. Open it with File → New (Open a 3D model).
  • A message says a texture wasn't found, or that some areas can't be painted: Choose the image again in the New window. Areas such as faces without UVs can't be painted (Open a 3D model).
  • Unity won't launch PetaNuri3D after you moved or updated it: On the Unity side, choose the PetaNuri3D location again (Choose the location again).
  • All the panels and the toolbar disappeared: They're hidden with Tab. Press Tab again to bring them back (Parts of the screen).
  • You can't find the panel you want, or the layout is messed up: Open the panel from Display → Windows, or reset the layout (Panel list, Reset the layout).
  • Transparent areas look gray or checkered, or low-resolution textures look blurry: These are display settings (How light and transparency look).
  • You can't update to a new version from inside the app: Get the ZIP from BOOTH and update by hand (Update to a new version).

Can't paint, or paint doesn't land where you want

  • Strokes leave no color, or only part of the area paints: The cause is a hidden or locked layer, a selection left over, or similar (Draw your first stroke).
  • You can't paint outside a selection, or can't paint where you want: A selection is still active. Deselect it, then paint (Selection).
  • Painting doesn't change the picture: You're set to draw on the layer mask (Layer mask).
  • Whatever color you pick, it only paints gray: You're still painting a paint target (shadow color, a mask and so on). Click "Back to color" to return (Paint target).
  • You can't add a layer (Lite): Lite allows up to 4 layers per texture set, including the base (Layers).
  • Painting jumps to another texture set: This is the function that switches automatically to the set where you start drawing. You can turn it off (Choose the set to draw on).
  • You can't paint over another texture set, or lines break at the neck seam: Check the setting for painting across two sets (Paint across two sets, When you can't paint).
  • You can't paint under clothes or inside hair: Hide the faces in front and you can paint what's behind (Visible area, Hide pen).
  • Part of the model stays hidden: You hid it. Press Alt+H to show everything again. Hiding isn't recorded in Undo (Visible area).
  • Paint also lands on the opposite side of a thick part: "Also paint back and hidden faces" is on (Also paint back and hidden faces).
  • You can't paint in Pose mode: See Pose mode.

Strokes, brushes or tools behave oddly

  • Pen pressure has no effect, the size doesn't change, or it feels off: Check in order, following When pen pressure doesn't work.
  • The pen doesn't respond after switching to WinTab, or "The pen tablet driver has no free WinTab slots" appears: See When pen pressure doesn't work.
  • The 3D view keeps rotating after you release the side button: See Pen buttons.
  • While you draw small details, the right-click menu appears by itself: See Right-click menu.
  • Keys don't work, or unexpected things happen: Check the key bindings (Key bindings).
  • Lines get thinner when you zoom in, stretch on slanted faces, or lag behind the pen: These depend on settings such as the brush size basis (Set how it feels).
  • Lines straighten or bend by themselves: They're snapping to a ruler (Ruler).
  • With symmetry, the other side is offset or doesn't paint: Check the symmetry center and the tools it doesn't work with (Paint symmetrically).
  • Fill leaks outside the lines, or a white gap is left at the edge of a line: Fix it with Close gaps and Expand/contract (Fill).
  • A stamp isn't placed, or even its white square gets placed: See Stamp.
  • A pattern appears upside down or sideways: See Pattern.
  • After typing text, pressing keys doesn't change the tool: You're still entering text (Text).
  • With the Move tool, "No visible layers are painted here" appears: See Move/transform tool.

Undo or effects behave oddly

  • Some operations can't be undone with Ctrl+Z: Some operations aren't recorded in Undo (Operations that can't be undone).
  • A filter changes nothing: See Filters.
  • Baked shadows are doubled or too dark: See Adjust after baking.
  • After Bake AO, the body under clothes turns completely black: See Bake AO.

Slow, freezing or save problems

  • It's slow, you can't paint, or "Display processing stopped" appears: Your GPU is running out of memory (Save memory).
  • The app crashed or froze: Reopen it and recover your work with File → Open from autosave (Save).
  • "The app's internal file access has stopped" appears, or the save dialog doesn't come back: See Save and resume.
  • A project file's name got "(incomplete)" added: Part of the file couldn't be read. Use Save as (Save).
  • A texture set got a ! mark, or your painting isn't visible after reloading: This is an orphaned set. You can move its layers to another set.

Export, Unity and AI integration

  • Lines are visible at UV seams in Unity or VRChat: Increase the bleed (padding) on export (Bleed).
  • You can't export (for example, "Can't export while the … is shown"): See Export as PNG.
  • The exported material is pink in Unity: See Export materials.
  • "Can't add any more" appears (paint target): See Paint target.
  • You painted from Unity, but nothing changes in the look: See Paint from Unity.
  • Your AI app says it can't connect: AI integration may be turned off (AI integration).

Contact us

If this manual doesn't fix the problem, or you want to send a request or bug report, you can send it from inside the app. The information needed to investigate (version, edition, environment) is filled into the message automatically.

  1. Choose Help → "Contact (support, feature requests, bug reports)".
  2. Choose a type. A place to write and a message that includes the version and other details appear.
  3. Fill in the blank fields.
  4. Click "Open the BOOTH shop (text is copied)" or "Open Discord (text is copied)". The text is copied and the destination opens.
  5. Paste the text at the destination and send it. For BOOTH, use "Contact the shop" at the bottom of the shop page. For Discord, join the server and go to the contact channel.
The status of the requests we've received is on the Requests and status page.
