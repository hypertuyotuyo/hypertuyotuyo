First steps: paint the sample model in about 10 minutes

Using the practice model "Shinobu", you will go through opening, rotating, drawing, symmetry, Fill, saving and exporting once from start to finish. You can do all of it in any edition, including the free Lite. Skip the fine settings: the goal of this page is simply to finish one run.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

What you need
  1. A Windows PC with PetaNuri3D installed (see Install and launch for how).
  2. A mouse (one with a wheel and a right button) or a pen tablet.
  3. You don't need your own model. The sample model comes with the app.
Steps on this page
  • 1. Open Shinobu
  • 2. Rotate and look around
  • 3. Add a layer to draw on
  • 4. Pick a color and draw
  • 5. Draw symmetrically
  • 6. Paint a large area with Fill
  • 7. Check it in UV
  • 8. Save and export the textures
  • Pages to read next

1. Open Shinobu

Open the practice model from the start screen.

  1. Launch PetaNuri3D.
  2. Click Try the sample in the middle.
  3. When "Shinobu" appears in the middle, you're ready.
  • The first time you launch, a guided tour (Tutorial) appears. You can follow it, or close it with "Skip" or Esc and continue on this page.
  • Shinobu is divided by body part into texture sets (the unit of one texture). The app automatically switches to the set where you start drawing, so you don't need to worry about it now.
The sample model may be used only to try PetaNuri3D. Do not extract it, distribute it or use it for anything else.

2. Rotate and look around

Move the view so you can see the area you want to paint.

The left button (pen tip) is for drawing. If you get lost, press F to see the whole model again.

If you'd rather use controls from another app, you can change them later with the "Camera control template" (Camera control template).

3. Add a layer to draw on

If you draw separately from the original picture (the base), you can delete just that layer when something goes wrong.

  1. Alt+right-click the part you want to draw on (for example, the hair). That part's texture set is selected and briefly lights up with a soft color.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+N. The leftmost button at the bottom of the Layers panel does the same.
  3. A new layer is added to the Layers panel and selected.
In Lite, you can have up to 4 layers per texture set, including the base. That is enough for this exercise.

4. Pick a color and draw

Trace over Shinobu's hair and ears with the pen.

  1. Press P to select the Pen.
  2. In the "Color" panel on the right, click a color you like.
  3. Trace over Shinobu's hair with the left button (pen tip).
  4. If you make a mistake, press Ctrl+Z to go back one step. Press Ctrl+Y to redo.
  • Press [ to make the line thinner and ] to make it thicker.
  • Alt+click on Shinobu to pick the color at that spot (Eyedropper).
  • To erase, press E to switch to the Eraser.

If it doesn't work

  • The line width doesn't change when you draw with the pen: Pen pressure is not arriving (see When pen pressure doesn't work).
  • You can't draw, or no line appears: In the Layers panel, check whether the selected layer is hidden or locked.

5. Draw symmetrically

When you draw on one side of the face, the same line appears on the other side.

  1. Press M. The X at the top left of the 3D view lights up.
  2. Draw on one side of Shinobu's face (the cheek, for example).
  3. The same line appears on the other side.
  4. When you're done, press M again to turn it off.

For detailed settings (front-back and top-bottom symmetry, how to set the center), see Paint symmetrically.

6. Paint a large area with Fill

Turn one part of the clothing into a single color in one go.

  1. Press G to select Fill.
  2. In the Sub tool panel, choose "Fill island".
  3. Click the clothing you want to paint. The connected part you clicked (a UV island) is filled with the foreground color.
  • If it fills more than you expected, press Ctrl+Z to undo. To fill only inside an area enclosed by lines, use "Fill enclosed area" (see Fill).

7. Check it in UV

See where your lines landed on the texture image.

  1. Press U. The UV (the unwrapped layout of the texture) appears next to the 3D view.
  2. Look at where the lines you just drew are on the UV. You can also draw directly on the UV.
  3. Press U again to close it.
Areas you can't paint because other faces overlap them, such as the inside of a collar, can be reached by tracing with "Hide pen" on the toolbar (the eye icon with a slash). It hides the faces in front so you can paint behind them. Press Alt+H to show everything again (see Hide pen).

8. Save and export the textures

PetaNuri3D has two operations: "Save", which keeps your work, and "Export", which creates the texture images.

  1. Press Ctrl+S to save to a project file (.petanuri). Your layers are kept as they are, so you can continue editing later.
  2. Choose File → Export → Export PNG (all sets).
  3. In the dialog that appears, just click "Export" and choose a destination.
  4. A PNG image is created for each texture set (the file name is the material name). The folder you exported to opens.

These PNGs are what you use in your avatar or game engine (Unity and so on). The project file is for continuing to paint in PetaNuri3D.
