Easy Bake, material look and shading Pro

PetaNuri3D calculates shading, gloss, weave and more from the shape of the model and paints them into layers. There are two ways to do it: "Easy Bake" makes a base coat for skin or clothing in one go, and "Bake" adds one effect at a time.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

This is a Pro feature. In Lite, the menu items show a lock icon. In Standard, they don't appear in the menu (Edition comparison).

Start with this
  1. Select the texture set you want to paint, then open Filters → "Easy Bake" (details).
  2. Under "Finish", choose skin, clothing, props or another type, and check the look in the 3D view (details).
  3. Click "Bake into a layer folder" to confirm, then add more paint on the "Paint details" layer inside the folder (details).
  4. To add only shadows or only gloss, use Filters → Bake and bake one effect at a time (details).

Create a base coat with Easy Bake

Easy Bake bakes shading and gloss suited to skin, clothing or props into layers all at once. It uses the base color and the original texture as the foundation and layers the effects on top.

  1. In the Texture sets panel, select the set to bake.
  2. Open Filters → "Easy Bake".
  3. Under "Finish", choose skin, clothing, props or another type. The 3D view shows how it will look after baking.
  4. Adjust "Overall strength", "Light horizontal angle", "Light elevation" and "Highlight amount".
  5. For skin, set where to add blush if you need it.
  6. Click "Bake into a layer folder". A folder named after the finish is created, containing one layer per effect and a transparent "Paint details" layer.

Window options

Start right away from New

On the New screen, check "Open Easy Bake after creation" and choose the target set. After the model opens, the Easy Bake window appears. It is unchecked by default. You can set the base color first and then try the finishes (New).

The preview is shown on a temporary layer. Click "Cancel" or the × on the window to close it without changing anything. After you confirm, a single undo removes the whole folder.

12 finishes

Each finish has its own set of effects (layers). All of them keep the original colors and layer the effects on top.

  • "Overall tint" uses Multiply. The other layers use Normal blending.
If you want the skin color to match the face, match the base color first. It is easier to adjust if you start with a light bake.

Add blush to cheeks and knees

With a skin finish, you can place the spots where you want redness yourself. A soft red tint spreads around each point.

  1. Choose a skin finish, then click "Add a location" under "Where to add blush".
  2. In the 3D view, click where you want redness, such as a cheek, elbow or knee.
  3. To move a point, drag the move handle in the 3D view or type the X, Y and Z coordinates.
  4. Adjust "Blush strength" and "Blush spread" while keeping the fair skin tone.
  5. When you bake, the blush becomes a separate layer named "Blush at selected points".
  • You can add as many locations as you like (up to 16). To remove a point, select it in the list and click "Remove selected location".
  • If you turn on "Add blush symmetrically", the same amount of blush is added on the opposite side. The center is the same as the center of Symmetry (X) used when painting. If you move the original point, the opposite one follows. It is off by default.
  • "Blush spread" is a ratio of the model's height.
This does not find cheeks or knees automatically. If you place no points, no per-location blush is added.

Bake one effect at a time (Bake)

Filters → Bake lists a bake command for each effect. You can bake just the effect you want to add after Easy Bake onto a new layer.

  1. Select the texture set to bake.
  2. Choose the effect you want from Filters → Bake.
  3. Pick a similar look from "Presets" and adjust the values. The 3D view shows the look immediately.
  4. Click "Bake to a new layer". A new layer is added to the selected set.

  • If you turn on "Apply to all visible texture sets", the effect is baked onto every visible set.
  • If there is a selection, only the inside of it is baked (only sets that have a selection are targeted).
  • You can rotate the view to check even during the preview. Click "Cancel" or the × on the window to close it without changing anything.
  • The preview is shown at a low resolution and is rebuilt at the set's resolution when you confirm. Check how fine weave and shading look after confirming too.
  • Cast shadows, thin-surface tint and AO are calculated after you choose the targets and quality. A progress display appears, and you can cancel partway.
What gets baked is texture color. Moving the view, or a light in Unity, after baking does not move the painted light or shadows. To check only the base color, set Shading to "Unlit (painted colors)".

Bake shading (soft shading, cavities and bulges, toon shade)

These bake shadows and brightness from the direction of the light and how the shape curves. None of them creates shadows cast by overlapping parts, so combine them with cast shadows if you need that.

Bake soft shading

Creates a soft shadow from the orientation of each face and a light you set. You set the light by its horizontal and vertical angle as seen from the front of the model.

  • Items: Light horizontal angle, Light elevation, Shadow range, Edge softness, Shadow strength, Shadow color.
  • Presets: Soft light from above and to the side, Shadows from overhead light, Shadows from side light, Crisp shadows.

Enhance cavities and bulges

Picks up how the shape curves and adds shadow to cavities and a light color to bulges. It does not pick up bump you painted (normal map).

  • Items: Detail width (ratio of the model's height), Cavity opacity, Bulge opacity, Shade blur, Edge softness, Cavity color, Bulge color.
  • Edge softness: Smooths the transition where the detected cavities and bulges turn into color.
  • Shade blur: Smooths the shading itself along the surface. Raise it if small jagged edges bother you. At 0, nothing is blurred.
  • Presets: Soft shape definition, Cavity shadows only, Bulge highlights only, Line art (fine line in cavities), Line art (thicker, softer), Ridge highlight (fine line on bulges).

Bake toon shade (1st, 2nd)

Splits the shadow into two crisp steps (1st shade and 2nd shade) according to the direction of the light. Use it as an underpainting for anime-style shadows.

  • Items: Light horizontal angle, Light elevation, 1st shade position, 2nd shade position, Edge blur, 1st shade strength, 2nd shade strength, 1st shade color, 2nd shade color.
  • Presets: Purplish 1st and 2nd shade / 1st shade only (sharp) / Warm skin shadow / From directly above.

Bake vertical and point gradients

Bakes colors that change with face orientation, height or distance from a point. Use it for bounce light from below or color changes toward hair tips and hems.

  1. Open Filters → Bake → "Bake gradients by orientation, height or point".
  2. Under "Color distribution", press one of Surface orientation, Height or Distance from point.
  3. Click the bar to add color stops, then set the color, position and opacity of each stop.
  4. Adjust the spread with "Boundary position", "Blend width" and "Color opacity", then click "Bake to a new layer".
Color distributionHow the color changes
Surface orientationFrom downward-facing faces to upward-facing faces. Use it for bounce light from below or to brighten upward-facing faces
HeightFrom the bottom of the model to the top. Use it for color changes from the hem or hair tips
Distance from pointFrom a chosen center outward. With "Pick center in 3D view", you can place the point on the surface of the model or in empty space. Move it with the move handle or by coordinates
  • "Spread distance" for Distance from point is a ratio of the model's height. You can't confirm until you place a point.
  • There are 240 presets, which you can search by group or name. Click "Save current" to add your own gradient to My presets.
  • "Use the drawing tool gradient" copies the colors of the Gradient tool. The bar works the same way as in gradients in the drawing tools.

Bake rim light, gloss, weave and angel ring

These bakes add material feel using color. Each one is calculated from the light and view direction you specify and is baked onto a new layer.

Bake rim light

Adds light color to faces near the silhouette. Items: Light horizontal angle, Light elevation, View horizontal angle, View vertical angle, Spread, Color opacity, Color. Presets: Subtle rim light, Thin blue rim, Warm backlight.

Bake gloss highlights

Adds bright gloss. Raising "Spread" gives a soft gloss, and lowering it gives the thin gloss of a hard material. Presets: Soft skin highlights, Satin highlights, Hard-surface highlights.

Bake weave and color variation

Choose weave or color variation with "Pattern", then set Pattern size, Pattern number, Color opacity and Color. Changing the number changes how the pattern appears. Size is a ratio of the model's height. Presets: Broad color variation, Fine weave, Fine leather variation.

Weave follows the axes of 3D space and the face orientation, not the UV. If you want to set the weave direction for each sleeve or hem, use the pattern pens. These bakes are color effects only. They don't create a normal map or reflection settings.

Bake angel ring (hair highlight)

Adds a ring-shaped band of highlight at a height you choose on the selected texture set (hair). It appears only on faces turned toward the view direction.

  • Items: Height (from top), View horizontal / vertical angle, Band width, Jaggedness, Number of jags, Edge softness, Shape number, Color opacity, Color.
  • Presets: White jagged ring, Smooth ring, Thin, sharp ring, Light blue gloss.

Bake cast shadows and thin-surface tint

Bake cast shadows

Creates shadows cast by hair and clothes from the overlaps in the current pose. You can use them together with shading layers.

  1. Open Filters → Bake → "Bake cast shadows".
  2. Set Light horizontal angle, Light elevation, Shadow softness, Shadow strength, Shadow color, Shadow distance (cm) and Quality.
  3. For each texture set, choose "Receive shadows" or "Cast shadows". For example, you can bake shadows onto the body and have hair and clothes only cast shadows.
  4. Click "Bake to a new layer".

Bake thin-surface tint

Probes inward from each face and adds color to thin spots where the opposite face is close by. It adds a soft translucent tint to closed shapes such as ears and fingers, and to thin props.

  1. Open Filters → Bake → "Bake thin-surface tint".
  2. Set "Thinness range (cm)", "Tint strength", "Translucent tint" and "Quality".
  3. Check "Bake" for the sets to bake, then click "Bake to a new layer".
A single open sheet of cloth is not a target for the translucent tint. If there is another face on the inside, it also picks up the distance to that face, so check the result and adjust the strength. This is not a material setting that actually lets light pass through.

Adjust and save after baking

Baked effects are ordinary paint layers. You can change their strength and range later.

  • You can lower a layer's opacity, hide effects you don't need, narrow the range with a mask, or erase parts with the eraser.
  • Easy Bake puts each effect on its own layer. This lets you, for example, weaken the shadows and keep only the blush.
  • If you paint on the "Paint details" layer, you can keep painting without disturbing the baked effects.
  • You can undo a bake with Ctrl+Z.
  • If you save to a project file (.petanuri), the baked layers are kept as they are. PNG exports the visible effects merged into one image, and PSD exports with the layers kept separate (Export).

If it doesn't work

  • Shadows are doubled or too dark: Baking again adds new layers. Hide or delete the old effect layers and compare.
  • Shadows don't move when I rotate the view: Baked shadows are texture color, so they don't move. To see shadows that move with the light, use the Final look display.
  • I can't confirm the distance-from-point gradient: Place a point with "Pick center in 3D view", then bake.
  • No blush is added: With a skin finish, click "Add a location" and place a point. If there are no points, no per-location blush is added.
  • The pattern detail differs between the preview and the result: The preview uses a low resolution. Adjust it by how it looks after confirming.
  • Only some sets are baked: If there is a selection, only sets that have a selection are baked. Hidden sets are also excluded.
  • It isn't in the menu, or it has a lock icon: This is a Pro feature.
