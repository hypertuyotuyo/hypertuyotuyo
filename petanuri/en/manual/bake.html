Easy Bake, materials and shading Pro

Calculate shading, highlights and fabric texture from the model's shape and paint them onto layers. Use Easy Bake for a complete starting finish, or Bake to add effects individually. Available since v1.1.0.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Lite, Standard, Pro, and the Pro upgrade are available on BOOTH.

Create base shading with Easy Bake

  1. Open a model and select the texture set to process. Your base color or original texture remains the foundation.
  2. Filter → Easy Bake opens the settings. Choose a skin, outfit or accessory preset under “Finish.”
  3. Check the “Preview,” then adjust “Overall strength,” “Light horizontal angle,” “Light elevation” and “Highlight amount.” Finishes with fabric texture or color variation also show “Surface detail.”
  4. For skin finishes, optionally specify Where to add blush.
  5. “Bake into a layer folder” applies the finish. Layers for each effect plus a transparent “Detail painting” layer are grouped in one folder, ready for further painting.
On the new-project screen, you can also enable “Open Easy Bake after creating” and select target sets. It is off by default. The settings open after creation. Previews use temporary layers, replaced with permanent layers when applied.

12 finishes

  • Skin: Fair/healthy blush, warm blush, soft illustration or deep shading. Combines soft shadows, cavity shading and highlights, with an overall tint for some finishes.
  • Outfit: Soft fabric, satin style, denim style, leather style or subtle color variation. Combines fabric texture, color variation, highlights and rim light to suit the material.
  • Accessories: Hard materials. Adds cavity shading, crisp highlights and rim light.
  • General: Warm light or blue rim light. Adds colored lighting to skin, outfits and accessories.

Presets layer effects over the original colors. To match skin to the face, match the base color first and start with a light bake for easier adjustment.

Where to add blush

Choose where to add redness, such as cheeks, elbows or knees. Soft blush surrounds each point. Adjust “Blush strength” and “Blush spread” while retaining fair skin.

  1. Choose a skin finish, click “Add a location” and pick a point in the 3D view.
  2. Move the selected point by dragging its 3D move handle or entering X/Y/Z coordinates.
  3. Repeat “Add a location” for more points, up to 16. Select unwanted points in the list and click “Remove selected location.”
  4. Check spread and strength, then bake. Blush becomes a separate “Blush at selected points” layer whose opacity you can adjust later.
Cheeks and knees are not detected automatically. With no points, no local blush is added. “Blush spread” is a proportion of model height.
Enable “Add blush symmetrically” to bake the same blush strength on the opposite side. It shares the X symmetry center used for painting. Moving the original point moves the opposite marker too; points on the center or already paired are not duplicated. Supports up to 16 points and their mirrors. Off by default.

Individual bakes and previews

To use effects individually, open them from Filter → Bake → each function.Bake AO is also in this submenu.

  • Shading, gradients, cavities, rim light, highlights and fabric texture preview immediately as settings change. They apply to the selected visible set. “Apply to all visible texture sets” applies them to multiple sets.
  • Rotate the view to inspect a preview. “Bake to a new layer” applies it; “Cancel” or the window's × restores the original.
  • Previews use a lower resolution and are regenerated at the set's resolution when applied. Check the final look after applying detailed fabric or shading effects.
  • Cast shadows, thin-area translucency color and AO are calculated after choosing targets and quality. Progress is shown and you can cancel midway.
Bakes write texture colors. Moving the view or lights in another app afterwards does not move the painted light or shadows. Use unlit painted-color mode to inspect only the base colors.

Soft shading, cavities and ridges

Bake soft shading

Create soft shadows from face normals and the chosen light. Set direction with light azimuth/elevation, then adjust shadow range, edge softness, strength and color. Start with presets for angled overhead, overhead, side or crisp shadows.

This alone does not create shadows cast by overlapping hair or clothes. Combine it with “Bake cast shadows” for those shadows.

Enhance cavities and bulges

Detect surface curvature, adding shadows to cavities and light colors to ridges. “Detail width” is a proportion of model height. Set cavity/ridge color and strength separately.

  • Edge softness: Smooths the transition from detected cavities/ridges into color.
  • Shade blur: Smooths the shading itself along surfaces. Increase it to reduce fine jaggedness. 0 applies no blur.
  • Presets include soft volume, cavity shadows only and ridge highlights only.

Orientation, height and point gradients

“Bake gradients by orientation, height or point” lets you edit color and opacity stops, then choose the basis using the “Color distribution” buttons.

  • Surface orientation: Changes color from downward-facing to upward-facing surfaces. Useful for reflected light from below or brightening upward-facing areas.
  • Height: Changes color from bottom to top of the model. Useful for gradients from hems or hair tips.
  • Distance from point: Changes color outward from the chosen center. “Pick center in 3D view” places it on the model or in empty space. Move it using handles or X/Y/Z coordinates.

“Spread distance” is a proportion of model height. Adjust boundary position, blend width and color strength. Point-distance baking cannot be applied until a point is chosen.

Click the bar to add color stops and set color, position and opacity per stop. Browse 240 presets by group or name. Register your own gradient with “Save current.” Editing works the same as the drawing tool's gradient.

Rim light, highlights, fabric texture and color variation

  • Bake rim light: Adds light color near the silhouette based on chosen light and view directions. Adjust width with “Spread”; presets include blue rims and warm backlight.
  • Bake gloss highlights: Adds highlights based on chosen light and view directions. Increase “Spread” for soft highlights, decrease it for narrow hard-surface highlights. Skin, satin and hard-material presets are included.
  • Bake weave and color variation: Choose fabric texture or color variation with “Pattern” buttons; adjust size, seed number, color and strength. Changing the number changes the pattern. Size is a proportion of model height.
Fabric texture follows 3D axes and face directions, rather than UV lines. To choose weave directions manually for individual sleeves or hems, use the pattern pens too. These bakes add color effects; they do not create normal maps or actual reflection settings.

Cast shadows and thin-area translucency color

Bake cast shadows

  1. Open Filter → Bake → “Bake cast shadows.”
  2. Set light azimuth/elevation, shadow softness, strength, color, distance and quality.
  3. Choose “Receive shadows” and “Cast shadows” per set. For example, bake shadows onto body while hair and clothes only cast them.
  4. Click “Bake to a new layer.” Shadows use overlaps in the current pose.

Bake thin-surface tint

Checks inward from surfaces and colors thin parts with nearby opposite faces. Adds a soft translucent color effect to closed shapes with thickness, such as ears, fingers and accessories.

  1. Open Filter → Bake → “Bake thin-surface tint.”
  2. Set “Thinness range (cm),” “Tint strength,” “Translucent tint” and “Quality,” then enable “Bake” for target sets.
  3. Click “Bake to a new layer” to calculate.
An open single sheet of fabric is excluded. Other nearby inner faces also affect detected thickness, so inspect the result and adjust strength. This does not configure actual light transmission in materials.

Adjust and save baked effects

  • Baked effects are regular paint layers. Lower opacity, hide unwanted effects, restrict them with masks or erase portions.
  • Easy Bake puts effects on separate layers, allowing adjustments such as weakening shadows but retaining blush. Add colors on the “Detail painting” layer to continue without altering the bake.
  • Undo a bake with Ctrl＋Z . Baking again adds new layers. If effects build up too much, hide or delete old layers to compare.
  • .petanuri preserves the generated layers when saved. PNG composites visible effects; PSD exports separate layers (Export).
