Bake AO Pro

Calculate darkness caused by nearby objects under arms, the chin or bangs, between fingers and in overlapping clothes, per texture set. Available in Pro.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

On this page
  • How to bake
  • Bake selected objects
  • Useful settings

How to bake

  1. Open Filter → Bake → Bake AO.
  2. In the table, choose sets to bake and sets that cast shadows (visible sets are selected initially).
  3. Choose objects to bake in the “Objects” column (All/None; click a name to briefly highlight it in 3D).
  4. Choose quality (ray count and samples within each texel) and shadow distance (Close 3cm, Normal 8cm, Wide 20cm or a custom value).
  5. Click “Bake.” An “AO” layer in Multiply mode is added at the top of each set.
Bakes the current pose and blend shapes. You can move the 3D view while baking. Canceling leaves everything unchanged.
To make the AO's white background transparent and recolor its shadows, use Convert luminance to opacity. To add soft shading from face direction or cast shadows from light, see Easy Bake, materials and shading.

Bake selected objects

  • Only the chosen objects' UV areas (plus a little padding outside the islands) are written. Other objects in the same set remain unchanged.
  • Choose “Calculate shadows from selected objects only” (default) or “Calculate shadows from the whole model” (unselected objects also cast shadows).
  • Selections are remembered per model.

Useful settings

  • “Don't receive other shadows”: useful when covered areas such as the body under clothes become completely black.
  • “Match model height”: scale distances in proportion to model size.
  • Adjust result strength and contrast to control darkness.
  • Cutout and translucent materials let light pass through transparent image areas, such as hair tips.
  • Export: write to lilToon's “Shadow AO” (included with material exports), or save PNG (<set name>_AO.png). If shadows are too strong, try rebaking with strength 0.6–0.8.
One Undo restores all sets.
