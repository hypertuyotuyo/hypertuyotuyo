Poses, blend shapes and vertex groups Pro

Use a 3D model's bones, blend shapes and vertex groups to paint areas that are hard to reach in a T-pose. You can lower the arms, change the expression or pick just part of the body.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

This is a Pro feature. In Lite, the buttons show a lock icon and the panels show only a description. In Standard, the "Paint/Pose" switch and the "Pose", "Blend shapes" and "Vertex groups" panels aren't shown (Edition comparison).

Start with this
  1. Click "Pose" at the top center of the 3D view, click a joint dot, and rotate with the gizmo that appears (details).
  2. Switch back to "Paint" and you can paint in that posed shape (details).
  3. To paint with a changed expression or body shape, move the sliders in the "Blend shapes" panel on the right (details).
  4. To paint only part of the body, pick it in the "Vertex groups" panel and click "Make selection" (details).

Pose the model and paint in that shape

Use this to paint spots that are hard to draw in a T-pose, such as the armpits and the inside of the arms. If you lower the arms first, the pen reaches more easily.

  1. Click "Pose" at the top center of the 3D view. You can't draw while in Pose mode.
  2. Click the joint dot of the bone you want to move. If you click the mesh, the bone that moves that spot the most is selected.
  3. Drag the gizmo that appears to rotate the bone.
  4. Click "Paint" to go back. The paint positions are rebuilt in the posed shape, and you can paint right away.
  • Other ways in: Display → Mode & pose → "Pose mode (move bones)", or right-click in the 3D view → "Enter pose mode (move bones)".
  • In the key bindings, you can assign a key to "Toggle paint/pose" (Assign keys).
  • The pose is saved in the project file. You can combine it with blend shapes.

The top bar in Pose mode

ButtonWhat it does
Spin / MoveSwitches between rotating and moving with the gizmo
Bone axes / World axesSwitches the gizmo's orientation between the bone's axes and the world's axes
ResetReturns the selected bone to its original pose
Reset allReturns the whole pose to the original
Symmetry / L→R / R→LLeft-right symmetric operations (Pose symmetrically)

The same operations are in the right-click menu in Pose mode too ("Select this bone", "Rotate (gizmo)", "Move (gizmo)", "Reset selected bones", "Back to paint mode" and more).

Type numbers in the "Pose" panel

  • You can also select a bone from the list in the "Pose" panel on the right. Use "Filter" to search for a name.
  • You can type the angle (X, Y, Z, in degrees) and the position of the selected bone as numbers.
  • Turn off "Weighted bones" and the bones that don't move the mesh are listed too.
  • Each drag of the gizmo and each typed entry can be undone.

Save and recall poses (Pose presets)

  1. In "Pose presets" at the bottom of the "Pose" panel, click "Save current state". The current pose is remembered with a name (up to 10).
  2. Change the name in the name field and press Enter.
  3. To use it, click "Load". Bones that weren't saved go back to their original pose.

"Overwrite" replaces a preset with the current pose, and × deletes it. One Undo reverts a load. Presets are stored in the project file.

If you paint with symmetry while posed, strokes land at the symmetric spot in the original pose. Even if you've bent an arm, you can paint the same spot on the other arm (Paint symmetrically).

If it doesn't work

  • You can't paint: You can't paint while in Pose mode. Click "Paint" at the top center to go back.
  • "This model has no bones" appears: A model without bones (an armature) can't be posed.
  • There's no "Pose" button: It isn't shown in Standard. It's a Pro feature.

Tell body, hand and swaying-part bones apart

In Pose mode, the joint dots change color and size by bone type. When you want to select an arm or a leg, you're less likely to click a hair or skirt bone by mistake.

Joint dotBones
Orange, largerThe humanoid body (hips, spine, chest, neck, head, shoulders, arms, legs, feet). The bone lines between body parts are orange too
GreenFingers
Blue, smallerEverything else (swaying parts such as hair, skirt, chest and tail)
  • If there's a humanoid bone near where you click, it's selected before other bones.
  • The list in the "Pose" panel gets the same color marks. Turn on "Humanoid only" to hide the swaying parts and show only the body and hands.
Whether a bone is humanoid is judged by its name. Depending on how the bones are named, a bone may be misjudged.

Pose symmetrically

Use this when you want to lower both arms the same way, for example.

  • "Symmetric posing": When it's on, moving a bone on one side moves the other side as its mirror image.
  • "Mirror L→R" and "Mirror R→L": Copy the pose of one side to the other side. Left and right are the character's left and right. One Undo reverts it.
  • You can use them from the top center row (Symmetry, L→R, R→L), the "Pose" panel, the right-click menu and Display → Mode & pose.
  • Left/right pairs are decided by name (Left/Right, .L/.R, _L/_R, a trailing L/R and so on). If the names don't tell, bones at mirrored positions in the original pose are paired.

If it doesn't work

  • "No left/right bone pairs found" appears: The model has no left/right clue in either its bone names or positions. Move one side at a time.

Change expressions or body shape and paint (Blend shapes)

You can paint with the model's blend shapes (shape keys) applied. Use this to paint spots that can't be seen in the original shape, such as closed eyes or the inside of the mouth.

  1. Open the "Blend shapes" panel on the right.
  2. If there are two or more meshes, choose a mesh from the dropdown at the top ("All meshes" is also available).
  3. Move a slider (0 to 100). When you stop, the paint positions are rebuilt in that shape.
  4. Paint as usual. Display, painting and hit testing all work in that shape.
  • Use "Filter" to find a name, and "Non-zero only" to show just the shapes you've moved. "All to 0" resets everything.
  • The values are saved in the project file.

Copy values and import .anim

  • "Copy values" copies the current (non-zero) values, and "Paste" restores them. You can paste them into another model too (other shape keys become 0).
  • "Load .anim" applies the values from the first frame of a Unity expression animation (.anim). Meshes are matched by name. You can also open the .anim as text, copy its contents and use "Paste" for the same result.
  • If there are shape keys that this model doesn't have, it tells you how many.

Display presets

Click "Save current state" in "Display presets" under the panel to remember the shape key values and which objects are shown or hidden, all together (up to 10). "Load" restores them, and one Undo returns to the previous state. The Objects panel has the same list (Showing and hiding objects).

If it doesn't work

  • No shape keys are listed: You may have another mesh selected in the dropdown at the top. Try "All meshes".
  • Pasting changes nothing: This model has no shape key with the same name as the copied ones.

Paint only part of the body (Vertex groups)

Create the area to paint or show from bone weights (vertex groups). When you want to paint only the arms or only the torso, you don't have to outline the area by hand.

  1. In the "Vertex groups" panel on the right, check the groups you want. You can pick any number of them (Shift+click selects several at once).
  2. Click "Make selection". The area you can paint becomes just those groups.
  3. Paint as usual.
Button or itemWhat it does
Make selectionTurns the area of the chosen groups into a selection
Add / SubtractAdd to the current selection / subtract from the current selection
Show only this areaMakes a selection and shows only that area in 3D (Visible area)
By weight strength (smooth edges)The weight becomes the paint strength as is, which smooths the edges
ThresholdAreas with a weight smaller than this are left out
Filter / Deselect allSearch by name / deselect all the selected groups

For how to work with the selection (expand, feather, deselect and so on), see Selection.

If it doesn't work

  • "No faces are weighted to the selected groups" appears: Try lowering the "Threshold".
  • No groups are listed: This model has no vertex groups (bone weights).
