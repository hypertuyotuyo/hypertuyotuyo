UV view and visible area

Place UV (the unwrapped texture map) beside 3D and paint in either. Hide front areas with Visible area to paint the body under clothes or inside hair.

Screenshots show the Japanese interface. Menu names in the text follow the selected app language.

Lite, Standard, Pro, and the Pro upgrade are available on BOOTH.

Notes beginning with “v1.2.0:” on this page are a release preview. The currently available version is v1.1.2.

Show UV view

  • Three layouts: 3D only, 3D beside UV, UV only. From 3D only, use UV at the 3D right edge (U or View → UV) to show both. Drag the divider to resize.
  • When side by side, × at the UV top right closes UV (3D only); × at the 3D top right closes 3D (UV only).Shift＋U toggles UV only ⇔ side by side. Last layout is remembered.
  • Shows UVs for the selected texture set (name at top left), updating when the set changes.
  • Pens, brushes, erasers, polygon fill, fill, enclosed fill, gradients, shapes, stamps, eyedropper, blur and smudge also work in UV.
  • While using UV, faces under the brush circle in 3D glow red. While using 3D, a red dot marks the painting position in UV.
  • Wheel zooms; middle drag or Space + drag pans; the square in the UV guide fits the view. Lines shows UV lines; Island edges shows outlines (▼ sets color/opacity).
  • The ⟲/⟳ in the bottom row of the UV guide rotate in 15° steps (paint while rotated).
  • Header Symmetry X/Y/Z matches the 3D top-left toggles (Paint symmetrically).
  • When UV is narrow, header buttons that do not fit move into ⋯.
Paint area (all editions): at the lower left of the UV pane, choose In UV, In padding, or Whole. In UV paints only inside islands as before, In padding also covers the padding band around them, and Whole paints anywhere on the texture. Pens, brushes, and erasers follow it, as do shapes, fills, gradients, stamps, blur, and smudge (painting in the 3D view is unchanged).
The padding at the lower left (0–128px or all) is the same value as export padding. The Padding line button in the header shows a line that far outside each island. Filters → Create padding on a new layer puts only the island edge colors spread outward on a new layer (all editions).
v1.2.0: 3D/UV small window (all editions): with 3D and UV side by side, the button below × at the upper right puts that view in a small window (the other fills the area). “UV in a small window (3D full)” and “3D in a small window (UV full)” under ⋯ in the UV header do the same.
v1.2.0: The small window can sit anywhere in the app window, even over panels. Drag its header to move it and its edges or corners to resize. ⇄ swaps, ▣ returns to side by side, and ✕ closes the small one. The lock at the upper right fixes it (press again to unlock). When it is small, the UV paint area options fold and the guide shrinks; in 3D the navigator shrinks.
v1.2.0: Paint into padding outside islands in 3D too (all editions): when turned on in Tool properties for pens, brushes, and erasers, strokes painted in 3D are extended outside the UV islands by the padding width when you finish (never into neighboring islands; one undo reverts the stroke). The width, Padding width (px), is shared with the lower left of the UV pane and export padding.
v1.2.0: Paint from UV edges (Pro): Filters → Paint from UV edges (new layer) creates a line or gradient in the foreground color on a new layer, by distance from UV island edges. Choose the shape (line or gradient), the side (inside islands, outside islands, or both), and distances in texture px. With a selection, only inside it.

Grid and snapping

  • Grid in the UV header toggles grid lines. Adjacent ▼ chooses subdivisions (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128 per texture side) and snapping. Pixels per cell also appear there.
  • Lines are display-only and excluded from artwork, saves and exports.
  • “Snap move/transform to grid” (enabled by default, only with grid visible): when moving a UV frame for Move/transform, Transform (Ctrl＋T) or Paste image on UV, corners/center snap near grid lines. Corner/edge handles, free-transform corners and warp points snap too.
  • Ctrl temporarily disables snapping while held. 3D frames do not snap.

UV guide (zoom/move)

A navigator similar to the 3D one appears at UV top right. Operate entirely with a pen.

  • Magnifier: drag vertically to zoom (up zooms in).Hand: drag to pan.Square: frame all.
  • Move using the top handle; resize at the left/bottom edges or bottom-left corner.
  • — collapses to a small circular icon; click to restore (also View → UV → UV guide).
v1.2.0: Rotation (all editions): use ⟲, the angle, and ⟳ in the guide’s bottom row (⟲/⟳ rotate 15°, clicking the angle resets to 0°, Shift+wheel rotates 5°). For any angle, drag the guide’s rotate button left or right (double-click for 0°) or drag the pane while holding Space+Shift.
v1.2.0: Zoom presets (all editions): Ctrl+1/2/3 set the UV pane (or the 2D canvas if open) to preset zoom levels (100%, 200%, 50% by default; 100% means one texel per screen pixel). Change them under Zoom presets (UV / 2D) in File → Preferences → Controls.

Beyond island edges (seam-continuous painting)

  • While painting in UV, a brush circle extending beyond an island will not paint another island merely adjacent in UV but distant in 3D.
  • In the header, “Paint across seams” (on by default) continues excess strokes beyond an island's seam onto connected faces in 3D without breaks. Off paints only within the current island (same toggle in context menu and View → UV).
  • With the paint area set to In UV, outside islands (unused UV areas) the brush is a faint dotted circle with ×. Strokes started outside begin painting upon entering an island. Text, shapes, stamps and gradients can be placed from outside islands.

Visible area (show/hide selected)

  1. Select islands/faces in UV or 3D (island/face selection, lasso, vertex groups etc.).
  2. Use the Visible area icon at the 3D top left, Shift＋H or Show selected only in the UV header to show only selected areas in 3D.
  3. H (Hide selected in the UV header) hides selected areas; add more repeatedly.
  4. Alt＋H returns to Show all.
  • After Show selected only, changing the selection automatically updates visibility (click again to show all).
  • Hiding deselects the area. Paint visible areas directly. Restore the deselected area using Restore selection below 3D or Ctrl＋Shift＋D undoes it.
  • 3D right-click → Hide this island / Show only this island directly hides/shows the clicked island (selection and Undo history remain unchanged).
  • Visible area icon ▼: Left has From selection (Show selected only, Hide selected, Show selected too, Hide pen) and Hidden areas (Invert, Show all, Expand/Shrink, Show faintly). Right has Visibility presets (saved list). Use this when nothing is hidden.
Hidden parts do not block depth, so you can paint surfaces behind clothing or inside hair. Hidden parts cannot be painted directly or picked by eyedroppers or fills. With the hidden-face symmetry option enabled, 3D painting can also reach the hidden mirrored side. You can hide entire objects (meshes) too (Show/hide objects).
Hiding/showing is excluded from Undo (Ctrl＋Z) history. Restore with Show all or a saved visibility preset.

Hide pen and the bar below 3D

Trace areas to hide them directly, useful for painting behind overlapping cloth.

  1. Choose Hide in the left toolbar (or toggle Hide pen in the bar below 3D).
  2. Choose Hide faces (quads hide both triangles together) or Hide islands (whole UV islands) as the sub tool.
  3. Traced 3D areas disappear. Clicking in UV hides them too.
  4. Click Hide pen again to return to the previous tool.
One stroke does not hide newly revealed faces behind the front. Trace again to hide them too.

The bottom-center bar below 3D appears only while something is hidden or the Hide pen is active. Shows Hide pen, Faces/Islands, Visibility presets ▼, current status (Preset: name / Partially hidden, unsaved preset / Showing selected only) and Show all.

Dim surroundings

  • While hidden, Show surroundings faintly below 3D displays hidden areas translucently so you can paint with context.
  • The adjacent slider adjusts opacity (5–80%). Also available under Hidden areas → Show faintly in ▼.
Ghosted hidden parts cannot be painted directly. If hidden-face symmetry is enabled, painting the opposite side in 3D also paints the hidden side.

Save visibility presets

Name and save frequently used visibility states to recall them anytime.

  1. Hide the desired areas.
  2. Below 3D, Visibility presets ▼ (or Visible area ▼) → Save current visibility; enter a name (matching names overwrite).
  3. Click a name in the same list to restore that visibility.
  • Manage visibility presets (rename/delete) changes names or deletes them.
  • Saved and current visibility states are stored in .petanuri projects and remain available after reopening.

Mesh lines and seam lines

  • Use top-left 3D icons (View → Lines and rulers → Mesh lines / Seam lines) to show polygon edges and UV seams/island edges. Toggle seam lines with Alt＋W too.
  • ▼ below the icon sets opacity, color (blue/black/white) and whether to show back/hidden lines.
