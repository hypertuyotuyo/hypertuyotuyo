Ask AI

Paste the PetaNuri3D guide into your AI chat, and you can ask that AI how to use the app. Ask in your own words, such as “How do I do X?” or “Which edition has this feature?”

Copy the guide

Covers v1.2.0 · updated 2026-10-11

The guide is in English. You can ask the AI in your own language.

Save as a text file

How to use

  1. Copy the guide with the button above.
  2. Paste it into the input field of your AI chat (Ctrl+V).
  3. Then write your question and send it.

If the AI says the text is too long to paste and it accepts attachments, attach the file saved with “Save as a text file.”

If your AI can read URLs, you can also give it this URL.

https://hyper-tuyotuyo.com/petanuri/llms-full.txt

Example questions

Click one to copy it. Paste it after the guide.

Please note

  • AI answers can be wrong. Before anything important, check the manual as well.
  • This guide covers v1.2.0. If your PetaNuri3D is newer (Help → “About PetaNuri3D”), check whether the guide on this page has been updated too.
  • Do not paste personal information, or other people’s model or asset files, into an AI.
  • If the AI says it does not know, or for bugs, contact us from Help → “Contact (support, feature requests, bug reports)” in the app.
