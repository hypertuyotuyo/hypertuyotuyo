03 — FEATURES

What you can do

A tool for painting 3D model textures as naturally as a 2D illustration.

Paint directly on 3D models

Rotate the view and draw with a pen where you see it. Your strokes go straight onto the UV texture.

Reach back and hidden faces

Every brush reaches hard-to-see areas, such as inside hair and overlapping clothes. Hide the front to paint behind it.

Paint without searching the UV map

Place the UV view beside the model and paint in either view. Lines and blur remain continuous across seams.

Move artwork on the model

Move, scale, rotate, free-transform and warp using the frame on the model. Type text right there, too.

Operate with just a pen

Supports pen pressure. Rotate the view with a side button, and switch colors and brushes using a palette that opens at the cursor.

Paint symmetrically

Press M for left-right symmetry. Combine X, Y and Z symmetry, including when painting in the UV view.

Launch from Unity and paint

Select an avatar in Unity and choose “ぺたぬり3D で塗る” (Paint with PetaNuri3D). Saving updates Unity. Original files are preserved (all editions).

PSD round trips with layers intact Standard

Import and export PSDs preserving 26 blend modes, folders, masks and adjustment layers.

Preview the lilToon look Pro

Paint with shadows, rim light, matcaps and outlines in a beta preview, then export materials as .unitypackage.

Paint in a pose and bake AO Pro

Pose the bones and paint in that shape. Calculate darkness where light is blocked and turn it into a layer.

04 — SCREEN

Interface and workflow

Here are four commonly used screens. For detailed instructions, see the Manual.

3D × UV

Paint in 3D or UV, side by side

Strokes in 3D immediately appear in the adjacent UV view. Move a brush over the UV view to highlight the matching model area in red.

  • Use pens, brushes, fill, gradients and blur on the UV map too
  • Show a UV grid and snap move/transform frames to it
  • Use “Show selected only,” the Hide pen and per-object visibility to paint under clothes and inside hair
LAYERS

Layers work like a 2D illustration

Supports clipping, transparency lock, folders, layer masks and 26 blend modes. Text remains in editable text layers.

  • Export textures to PNG, or PSD to keep the layers Standard
  • Layer strokes, adjustment layers and filters remain editable Standard
  • Import an avatar's customization PSD and continue painting Standard
  • Export padding helps prevent visible seam lines
BRUSH

Brushes for anime coloring and painterly art

From G-pens, mapping pens and tapered pens to watercolor bleed, ink brushes and painterly brush collections. Blend, smudge and blur directly in 3D.

  • 5 pens, 12 brushes, 33 brush-collection presets, 12 blend tools and 13 decoration tools
  • 15 pattern pens, stamps and shapes for clothing patterns. A preview shows where they will land before painting
  • Surface relief tools for seams and wrinkles Pro
POSE

Paint with poses and blend shapes Pro

Rotate bones to pose the model, then paint in that shape. Paint with facial blend shapes applied, too.

  • Select bones using joint points, the mesh or the list, then rotate with handles
  • Create painting selections from vertex groups (bone weights)

08 — FAQ

FAQ

Where can I buy it?
BOOTH product page offers all editions. Lite is free, Standard is ¥2,500 and Pro is ¥4,000. During the launch sale through November 2, Standard is ¥1,500 and Pro is ¥3,000.
Can I use just the free Lite edition?
Yes. Lite includes everyday painting features: direct 3D painting, brushes, stamps, patterns, symmetry, UV view, selections, transforms, text, PNG export, project saving and launching from Unity. It allows up to 4 layers per texture set including the base, and resolution up to 2048. There is no watermark.
Can I use it for VRChat avatars?
Yes. Open avatars, outfits and accessories (FBX, VRM and more) used in VRChat and paint textures directly in 3D. Export PNG or PSD textures to Unity. Select an avatar in Unity and choose “ぺたぬり3D で塗る” (Paint with PetaNuri3D) to launch the app; saving updates Unity (all editions). Pro can export lilToon materials as .unitypackage.
Will it overwrite the original avatar files?
No. Even when painting from Unity, original FBX, materials, textures and prefabs stay unchanged; painted assets go into Assets/PetaNuri/ in the Unity project. When used on its own, PetaNuri3D only reads the model file.
Can I use it without other 3D or painting software?
Yes. PetaNuri3D alone can open a model, paint it and export PNG textures. To continue in another painting app with layers intact, export PSD (Standard and above).
Can I upgrade to Pro after buying Standard?
Yes. Install the file from BOOTH's “Upgrade to Pro” into Standard and restart to activate Pro. Projects and settings are preserved. For details, see Manual.
Does it work with avatars and outfits from other shops?
You can paint 3D models such as FBX files if they have UVs (texture coordinates). Please check each model's terms of use.
Can I use it on Mac?
Currently Windows only (Windows 11; also runs on Windows 10, which is not supported).
“Windows protected your PC” appeared
As the app is unsigned, this may appear on first launch. Choose “More info” → “Run anyway” to start it.Getting started has more information.
How do I find out about new versions?
At startup (at most once a day), the app checks for new versions and notifies you at the bottom right. It checks only the version number; your artwork and settings are never sent. Latest information is also available in the X(@Yak_HyperTYTY).
